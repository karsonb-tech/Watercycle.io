Evaporation

Evaporation is the process of water getting heated up and turned from liquid to a gas and it becomes water vapor
It works by when water gets heated it becomes water vapor.
Things that increase the speed of evaporation can be higher temperature, less humidity, more wind speed, and more surface area.
It matters since it's part of the water cycle and it can make clouds that cause shade and cools things down.

Infiltration

Infiltration is the process of water going into ground.
It works by water starting on the surface of the ground and soaking into the ground reaching deeper and deeper.
It works better on surfaces that are more permeable like mulch since it has more ways to flow into, so if it has a good permeable surface and that has ways to flow into there will be more infiltration.
This matters since if it goes into soil it can hydrate plants, stop flooding, and if it goes on to an impermeable surface there can be evaporation.

Transpiration

Transpiration is the process of water vapor coming out of stomata and leaves.
It works by carbon dioxide entering the stomata while water vapor and oxygen leaves.
Things that increase transpiration are heat.
It matters because we get oxygen from the process and that is something humans need.
